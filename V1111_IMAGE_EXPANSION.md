# v1.11.1 — Visual pilot expansion

This update does two things: (1) recalibrates the Hard tier of Flags of the World toward recognizable countries (Greece, India, Mexico, South Korea), and (2) adds Famous Landmarks as a second image-only year-round category with 12 regular questions and one Final Word.

Rationale: the first playtest indicated that the original Hard flags behaved more like obscure deep cuts than appropriately difficult recognitions. The revised set should still feel harder than the Easy and Medium tiers without becoming guess-only. The landmark category is intentionally broad and recognizable so the app can test a second family of visuals, a broader range of image shapes, and the TV/phone presentation of landmark scenes.

All new images are original vector illustrations stored locally in `public/trivia-images/`. No additional Supabase changes are required beyond v1.10.0.
