# v1.13.13 — Picture This: Famous Landmarks

Replaces the illustrated Famous Landmarks pilot in place (same category ID `famous-landmarks`) with 15 actual user-supplied photographs, five per difficulty, and two distinct photographic Final Word questions. All 24 supplied JPEGs were inspected; 15 landscape and 9 portrait, no low-resolution replacements required. Seven are reserved. Assets are optimized at up to 1600 pixels on the long side, with orientation corrected and metadata removed. Original source handles and photo IDs are recorded in IMAGE_CREDITS.json and the audit CSV. Pexels general license was reviewed; exact photo pages and any separate depicted-content rights have not all been independently verified. Sydney Opera House and Christ the Redeemer are reserved pending additional rights review.

The category's existing disabled state remains in force. After deployment, open Developer > Category Availability and enable Picture This: Famous Landmarks when ready. No SQL changes. Both finals are excluded when the category appears in either regular round or is disabled. The selection engine prefers unseen questions, then existing exposure rules; equal-priority finales are drawn by category first so multiple finals do not multiply a category's chance. The required minimum counts distinct eligible categories. The previous Machu Picchu final was replaced, resolving the known duplicate-answer test failure.

The library now contains 76 categories, 999 regular questions and 77 category-linked Final Word records (69 eligible before season and disabled filters; eight archived). Total stored category question records: 1,076. Older pilot SVGs remain unused in the repository, so no deletions are required. Some retired question IDs are not in the new active bank; deploy between shows rather than while a room is active. Previous exposure rows remain in Supabase; reused pilot IDs correspond to the same named subjects, but their images/distractors may differ, so interpret historical pilot statistics cautiously.

The approved full-bleed icon replaces the 192px and 512px home-screen assets and adds an opaque 180px Apple touch icon. The original main logo is unchanged. Existing installed icons may be cached; remove and re-add the home-screen shortcut if needed. No environment changes.

Validation: 120/120 engine tests, 72/72 UI contract tests and strict game-type check passed. Every active photograph was decoded and its dimensions verified. Full Next.js production build and real-device visual testing were not performed in this environment.
