THE KNOW SHOW — v1.8.4 · COMPLETE CUMULATIVE APP

Deploy the complete app from this ZIP directly over any earlier version (including v1.7.0 or v1.5.4). No intermediate installations required. Existing Vercel environment settings and Supabase deployment remain unchanged.

NEW IN v1.8.4: Slightly more landing-page breathing room on desktop and mobile, with compact spacing on short phones.

FROM v1.8.3: Gold underlined How to play link in the hero’s top-right, mobile-safe form font sizes, and a clearer, larger Green Room answer-choice slider. No gameplay or database changes.

FROM v1.8.2: Compact landing page on phones and desktops, no duplicate banner, centered How to play, new 1,000-question tagline, and no default hosting passcode field. Configured hosting passcodes are requested only when required; player join codes still work.

FROM v1.8.1: Eight category offers per round for every player/team count; choose four. At least one eligible seasonal offer is guaranteed when available, and other in-season categories can compete for the remaining spots. Round 2 excludes Round 1 selections. No seasonal selection cap. Existing weighting and question banks remain intact.

FROM v1.8.0: Three categories (The No-Show!, Christmas Legends & Lore, Creepy Creatures), 48 regular questions and 3 reserved Final Word questions. Library now: 72 categories, 942 regular questions, 72 Final Word questions; 1,014 questions total. The homepage and short How to Play guide promote over 70 categories and over 1,000 questions.

REVIEW: See V180_REGULAR_REVIEW.csv, V180_FINAL_WORD_REVIEW.csv and V180_AUDIT_NOTES.md for question accuracy, choices, hints, links and documented review limits.

GITHUB / VERCEL DEPLOYMENT
1. Unzip this archive, open the inner the-know-show folder, and place the contents (not the folder itself) at the ROOT of your existing GitHub repository.
2. Commit changes to main; connected Vercel will trigger a build and deployment.
3. Check the build result, refresh the PWA, and playtest with at least two devices.
4. Never upload secrets to GitHub; existing Vercel env vars remain there. No database schema migration or new variables are required.

The approved logo PNG and app icons are unchanged. A source archive is not a substitute for a live Supabase data backup or verified production build.
