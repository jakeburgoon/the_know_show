THE KNOW SHOW — VERSION 0.2.0

This is a FULL replacement for v0.1.2, including revised gameplay and 12 Suno music embeds. COOL JAZZ is the default.

TO UPGRADE YOUR EXISTING GITHUB/VERCEL APP:
1. Open the extracted ZIP folder and select everything INSIDE it.
2. Upload those contents at the ROOT of your existing `theknowshow` GitHub repository.
3. Commit the changes and allow Vercel to deploy.
4. Create a brand-new room after the update; older rooms are not compatible with v0.2.
5. Scan the private host QR from the creator's laptop screen onto your host phone.
6. Open /display/ROOMCODE on the laptop and share that browser tab to the TV.
7. Start Cool Jazz playback by pressing Play in the TV's Suno player. Music playback/volume stay in Suno's player.
8. Players join from the public QR on TV and answer from their phones.

NO SUPABASE SCHEMA CHANGES ARE NEEDED if v0.1.2 is already working.

For more detailed guidance and technical caveats, read README.md.
