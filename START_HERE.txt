THE KNOW SHOW — VERSION 0.2.1 — HOST CAN PLAY

1. Unzip the file and open the extracted `the-know-show` folder.
2. Upload ALL contents into the ROOT of your existing `theknowshow` GitHub repository and commit. Vercel should redeploy.
3. Open the room on your laptop. If you are a host, switch to HOST CONTROLS; pair the phone using the private host QR if you haven't already.
4. On your phone, tap MY PLAYER VIEW, join with your name and age, and play normally. Tap HOST CONTROLS whenever you need admin tools; tap MY PLAYER VIEW to return to your questions and answers.
5. Tap TV PREVIEW to inspect the main display on the phone, then use the BACK button or the Player/Host tabs to return.
6. Open `/display/ROOMCODE` on your laptop and cast/share it to your TV. Start Cool Jazz from the TV's Suno player.

IMPORTANT:
- No Supabase changes; v0.2.2 rooms continue working.
- If your v0.2.2 phone lost its host token after joining, pair it again from the original creator's laptop. The new version saves both tokens.
- Full production build wasn't possible here; engine tests and local type-shape validation passed.
