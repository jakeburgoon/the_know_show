THE KNOW SHOW — v1.6.2 · COMPLETE CUMULATIVE APP

This full project replaces v1.6.1 or any earlier release in a single deployment.
No incremental patches, Supabase migrations, or new environment variables are required.

NEW IN v1.6.2
- Homepage cards now lead with category variety, then the 25-question game,
  then hints and opportunities to double points.
- All earlier v1.6.1 hosting, music, and game improvements are included.

ALSO INCLUDED FROM v1.6.0
- Host setup has TWO quick choices: individuals or teams (individuals preselected),
  then Just phones or TV + phones (Just phones preselected).
- Every joined player's answer format is set with a four-position slider in the
  Green Room: all written, Hard-only choices, Medium/Hard choices, or choices always.
- Music is no longer a setup question. The host's player opens when the show begins
  in Just phones mode, with a brief banner pointing to the top-right music menu.
- The Green Room now has a shorter “Additional settings” section without redundant
  soundtrack arrangement controls.
- The homepage and How to Play guide both describe TWO setup choices.

GITHUB / VERCEL
1. Unzip this archive. Open its inner the-know-show folder.
2. Replace the corresponding application files at the ROOT of your existing GitHub repository (not a second nested folder). Commit to main.
3. Your connected Vercel project should build and deploy. Check the Vercel build log for errors.
4. Open a new game room and playtest on two phones before a larger show. Camera scanning requires HTTPS and camera permission. Refresh the PWA if it still shows an old version.

NO SUPABASE MIGRATION OR NEW ENVIRONMENT VARIABLES. Existing server-side secrets stay on Vercel and must not be committed.

INCLUDED FROM v1.5.9 AND EARLIER
- 66 additional seasonal questions: 6 Easy, 6 Medium, 6 Hard per seasonal category, 738 regular questions total. Full additions and review notes in SEASONAL_QUESTION_EXPANSION_QA.csv.
- Weighted, rather than exclude-and-rank, category offers; recently played categories can return, with lower probability. One eligible seasonal category is still offered every Build the Board phase.
- Small header music menu. Players may change the shared soundtrack, unless the host disables this option. Music plays from host phone or TV, not everyone's device; opening the player/pressing Play can still be needed.
- All prior improvements, including Final Word and branded screens, remain included. The approved logo and app icons are unchanged.

ALSO INCLUDED FROM v1.5.8
- When everyone actually answers incorrectly, a brief branded “A TOTAL NO-SHOW!” animation precedes the normal answer, individual results, and leaderboard.
- No change to the scoring rules or any question.
