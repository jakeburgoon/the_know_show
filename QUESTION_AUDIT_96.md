# v0.2.4 — Detailed accuracy and editorial audit of the 96 new question-set items

This review inspects all **96** audience-specific questions introduced in v0.2.3 and carried into v0.2.4, including answers, hints, ambiguity, accepted alternatives, difficulty progression, and duplicate risks.

- **96 inspected**; **82 retained**, **14 revised or replaced**.
- **549 total question records**, spread across 45 categories (the prior 453 + 96 new records). All 96 remain available after the revision.
- **14 amended items cross-checked against listed external sources** (sources link in the CSV). The remaining 82 passed an individual editorial factual-sanity review, **not exhaustive independent source-by-source verification**. Some URLs may later require revisiting, and sources should be rechecked before publication.
- **No known incorrect accepted answer remains among the 96**, based on this review; this is not a guarantee that all 549 questions are error-free.
- Automated checks confirm no exact duplicate prompts, answer-inside-prompt giveaways among the 96, and at least two audience-eligible alternatives for every category/level.
