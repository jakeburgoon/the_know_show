# The Know Show v1.13.3 — Gameplay flexibility and review breaks

## What changed
- Host View now includes a Player answer support section during a show. Change a contestant's setting without pausing. The choice format is fixed when a question appears, so active written drafts, multiple-choice validation, and scoring remain consistent. Changes apply on the next regular question; Final Word remains written. Team seats may also use this control. The age selector remains in the individual Green Room.
- Answer choices are described as a preference for balancing ages/abilities or enjoying a more relaxing game, rather than a remedial accommodation. Host startup tip now includes Host View settings, support, and score adjustments; it lasts 9.5 seconds instead of 6.5 and reappears on a rematch.
- Standard result time increases 7.5 → 8.5 seconds, all-miss 11 → 12 seconds, Final Word 13 → 14 seconds, and all-miss Final Word 16.5 → 17.5 seconds. The additional second is in the answer beat; standings and Total No-Show beat durations are unchanged.
- A player with an actual incorrect answer can request a review of the last regular question after returning to the board, during a new round draft, or at Final Word wagering. Review closes as soon as the next regular question is selected or Final Word begins. A request reopens the last result and pauses progression until the host reviews all outstanding requests. Once resolved, the existing board/draft/wagers resume. Denied requests cannot be repeated for the same answer. Corrected scores and aggregate question stats update without creating another exposure record. Final Word review remains available during its results screen until Curtain Call.
- The first question feedback click while results are still active grants a five-second shared break, server-enforced. Other feedback requests for that question do not extend the break. Feedback during a host pause or an existing dispute does not add a separate pause. A player may continue typing after the break; the dialog announces resumption and remains open until dismissed. If the result has already expired when the request arrives, feedback still works but cannot interrupt the next stage. Feedback never changes score. Show-end feedback has no pause.
